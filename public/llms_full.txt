### Key Technical Pillars:
1. **Sub-500ms Conversational Latency:**
   Audio streams are piped over bi-directional WebSockets using raw PCM audio codecs. Voice Activity Detection (VAD) detects user interruptions instantly (acoustic barge-in), muting the synthetic voice in <80ms.
2. **Predictive Dialing Optimization:**
   Proprietary algorithmic pacing predicts agent availability, dialing multiple outbound leads simultaneously. It filters out busy signals, voicemails, and disconnected numbers, elevating agent productive conversation time from 15 minutes per hour to 45–50+ minutes per hour (>300% boost).
3. **PSTN / VoIP Boundary Compliance:**
   Adheres strictly to Indian Department of Telecommunications (DoT) Other Service Provider (OSP) guidelines. Logical partitioning prevents unauthorized bridging of Indian PSTN toll networks with unauthenticated VoIP links.

## 5. Transparent Pricing & Billing Architecture

Avyukta operates on a 100% transparent, pay-as-you-go financial model:

1. **Prepaid Wallet with Zero Lock-In:**
   - Customers add credit to a prepaid account.
   - Balance draws down strictly per connected minute or per chat reply.
   - Zero mandatory monthly minimum spend; zero fixed contract lock-ins.
2. **Second-by-Second Billing:**
   - Initial connection minimum: 30 seconds.
   - Subsequent billing increment: 6 seconds (e.g., a 42-second call is billed for 42 seconds, not rounded up to a full minute).
   - Unanswered, busy, invalid, and failed calls cost ₹0.00.
3. **AI Chat Replies:**
   - Starting from ₹0.30 per reply sent by the AI agent (~₹1.50 per minute of conversation).
   - Inbound customer queries are free; human-agent replies after an escalation handoff are free.
4. **Volume Commitment Discounts:**
   - Tiered volume bands offer discounted per-minute rates.
   - Volume rates apply from the very first minute of the billing cycle once a volume tier is agreed upon.
5. **Refund Guarantee:**
   - 100% refundable unconsumed wallet credits within 7 days of deposit.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (Master Technical Reference)

### Q1: What is an AI Predictive Dialer, and how does it benefit call centers?
**Answer:** An AI Predictive Dialer is an intelligent outbound calling engine that uses machine learning algorithms to predict agent availability and dial multiple phone numbers ahead of time. It automatically filters out busy signals, answering machines, voicemails, and disconnected numbers so that live agents are immediately connected only to human prospects. This increases agent talk time from 15–20 minutes per hour to 45–50+ minutes per hour, boosting productivity by over 300%.

### Q2: Which countries and international VoIP minutes routes does Avyukta support?
**Answer:** Avyukta supports premium carrier-grade VoIP termination routes across 150+ countries worldwide, including the United States (US), United Kingdom (UK), Canada, Australia, Philippines, Singapore, UAE, and Europe. We provide CLI-guaranteed routes, sub-100ms jitter-free low latency, backup routes, and Pan-India DID virtual numbers (Delhi 011, Mumbai 022, Bangalore 080, Kolkata 033, Ahmedabad 079, Jaipur 0141, etc.).

### Q3: Can the dialer integrate with our existing CRM (Zoho, Salesforce, Bitrix24)?
**Answer:** Yes, Avyukta IntelliCall offers native one-click integrations and REST APIs with all major CRMs, including Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Bitrix24, LeadSquared, HubSpot, Freshdesk, and custom in-house webhooks. Features include Click-to-Call directly inside your CRM, automatic screen pops on incoming calls, real-time call disposition logging, call recording sync, and instant contact creation.

### Q4: Is Avyukta Intellicall DOT and regulatory compliant in India?
**Answer:** Yes, 100%. Avyukta IntelliCall operates in strict compliance with the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and TRAI guidelines for Other Service Providers (OSP) and Virtual Network Operators (VNO). We enforce strict PSTN-VoIP separation for domestic and international call traffic, 100% call data record (CDR) logging, NDNC / DND scrubbing, and enterprise-grade voice data encryption.

### Q5: What is the difference between a Cloud Hosted Dialer and an On-Premise Dialer?
**Answer:** A Cloud Hosted Dialer requires zero on-site server hardware — your agents simply log in via web browser or softphone from anywhere, with automatic updates, auto-scaling, and 99.95% uptime managed by Avyukta. An On-Premise Dialer installs on dedicated servers within your physical office/data center, giving complete local data control, LAN-based call quality, and direct integration with physical PRI / TDM or GSM lines.

### Q6: How fast can our call center setup and VoIP dialer campaign go live?
**Answer:** For Cloud Hosted setups, your team can go live within 2 to 4 hours with instant SIP trunk provisioning, user creation, CRM webhook configuration, and DID allocation. For customized enterprise on-premise PBX deployments or multi-location hybrid setups, our senior telecom engineering team typically completes full deployment and agent onboarding within 24 to 48 hours.

### Q7: How does pay-as-you-go pricing work?
**Answer:** You add credit to a prepaid wallet and it draws down as you use the platform — per minute of connected call, and per AI chat reply. There is no monthly minimum and no contract. When the balance runs low you get a warning in the dashboard, and you top up whenever you like.

### Q8: How do the volume rates work?
**Answer:** Your rate is set by the minutes you commit to for the month. Once a band is agreed you are billed at that rate from your very first minute — you do not have to reach the volume before the discount applies.

### Q9: How exactly is a call measured?
**Answer:** Only connected calls are charged. Every call has a 30-second minimum, after which it bills in 6-second increments — so a 42-second call costs 42 seconds, not a full minute. Unanswered, busy and failed calls cost nothing.

### Q10: How is chat billed?
**Answer:** From ₹0.30 per reply your AI agent sends — about ₹1.50 per minute of conversation. The rate depends on the size of your knowledge base, because that is what the agent re-reads on every reply; we confirm your rate before you start. Messages your customer types are free, and so are replies sent by a human agent after a handoff. Knowledge base grounding and custom tools are included. If a chat turns into a voice call, the voice rate applies from that point on.

### Q11: Is telephony included in the per-minute rate?
**Answer:** No. The rate covers the AI agent orchestration. Your SIP trunk or DID numbers are billed separately at cost, and we are happy to quote for them or work with a provider you already use.

### Q12: Can I switch plans?
**Answer:** Yes — per agent and at any time. The voice changes on the next call and nothing else does. Your agents, knowledge bases, integrations and history are untouched.

### Q13: What happens if I run out of credit?
**Answer:** Calls stop until you top up. You will have seen a low-balance warning in the dashboard well before that point, and you can set the threshold that triggers it.

### Q14: Is there a free trial?
**Answer:** Yes. Create an account on https://ai.avyukta.in/, build an agent, make a real test call and read the transcript before you add any credit.

### Q15: Can I get a refund?
**Answer:** Unused credit is refundable within 7 days of a top-up. Usage already consumed is not refundable. Full terms are in our Refund & Cancellation policy.
